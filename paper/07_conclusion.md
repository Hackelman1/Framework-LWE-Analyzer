## 7. Conclusion and Future Work

This work has formally characterized the noise uniformization phenomenon in LWE modular projections $\mathbb{Z}_q \to \mathbb{Z}_m$ and extended its methodology toward the empirical statistical auditing of implementation-level transformations in NIST post-quantum standards **ML-KEM (FIPS 203)** and **ML-DSA (FIPS 204)**:

1. **Algebraic Uniformization Criterion**: Uniformization requires $\gcd(q, m) = 1$, which guarantees that the generated additive subgroup $G(q, m) = \langle q \rangle = \mathbb{Z}_m$ spans the full support of size $m$.
2. **Modular Wrapping Mechanism**: The integer wrapping quotient $k = \lfloor (A s + e)/q \rfloor \bmod m$ acts as a secret-independent stochastic mask, proving that uniformization is intrinsically driven by modular dynamics and occurs even in the complete absence of original noise ($e = 0$).
3. **Discrete Convolution Bound**: The circular convolution $P(e \bmod m) \circledast P(k q \bmod m)$ bounds the total variation distance of the effective noise by the distance of the wrapping distribution to uniform: $\delta(P(e_{\text{effective}}), U(\mathbb{Z}_m)) \le \delta(P(k q \bmod m), U(\mathbb{Z}_m))$.
4. **Comprehensive ML-KEM & ML-DSA (FIPS 203/204) Audit**: Comprehensive empirical evaluations across $N = 500,000$ samples under $B = 256$ fixed binning, $P = 500$ permutation tests (`seed = 42`), add-one smoothing (Phipson & Smyth, 2010), internal Bonferroni sweep aggregation ($\tilde{p}_m = \min(K_m \cdot p_{\min}, 1.0)$), and global Benjamini-Hochberg False Discovery Rate (FDR) control across $M = 23$ leakage hypothesis tests confirm zero statistical leakage. The commitment low-part residue ($r_0$ in `Decompose`), public key truncated residue ($t_0$ in `Power2Round`), and binary hint vector ($h$ in `MakeHint` / `UseHint`) preserve strict statistical independence regarding secret keys $S_1$ and $S_2$, achieving $I_{\text{MM}} = 0.000000$ bits of corrected mutual information, adjusted leakage $q$-values $> 0.50$, and passing marginal uniformity ($\chi^2\ p\text{-value} > 0.16$).

These findings close the mathematical characterization of modular projection effects in LWE and establish a rigorous, automated statistical framework for verifying that practical implementation operators in post-quantum standards introduce zero statistical secret leakage towards data-model adversaries.
